# Mutant Undergångens Arvtagare

Inofficial Roll20 sheet for Mutant Undergångens Arvtagare.

This sheet has not been approved by the owners of the Intellectual Property for Mutant Undergångens Arvtagare, and thus cannot be published as a Roll20 Community sheet. 

## How to use in a game

To use these files the GM has to have a Pro or higher subscription in Roll20, since only those levels have access custom character sheet option. 

In your Roll20 game, go to the Game settings and scroll to the bottom. In the dropdown list of character sheets, choose Custom. This will show the option to paste the content of the files in the respective textfield. The dod91.html goes into the HTML area, the dod91.css in the CSS, and the translation.json into the Translation one. 

> The checkbox for LEgacy sanitization needs to be cleared or else the sheet will not display properly

## How to use as a player

The GM will have provided edit access to a character in the Jounal tab, click it to open it. 

The sheet is divided into a few sections. At the top you have Attributes, Secondary or Derived Attributes as well as character information and details. Below it you have a section for skills, left side is the basic skills and to the right you have trained skills. Under skills you have the weapon section, and the bottom is divided between gear (including armor) and abilities.
